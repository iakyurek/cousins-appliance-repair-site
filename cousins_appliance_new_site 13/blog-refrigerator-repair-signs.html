Signs Your Refrigerator Needs Repair

From cooling issues to strange noises, here are the signs your refrigerator may need service.

Back to Blog

Cooling issues, frost buildup, leaks, and strange noises are signs to watch for.

Refrigerator Repair

What this usually means

Your refrigerator works all day and all night, so small issues can become expensive if they are ignored. One of the clearest signs of a problem is inconsistent cooling. If food is spoiling faster than normal or one section feels warmer than another, the refrigerator may not be holding the right temperature.

Other warning signs include leaking water, frost buildup, loud fan noises, a refrigerator that runs constantly, or an ice maker that suddenly stops working. These issues can come from several different parts, including fans, sensors, seals, drain lines, or cooling components.

A refrigerator problem can also lead to food waste, so it is usually better to get it checked early instead of waiting for the appliance to stop cooling completely.

A repair visit allows a technician to inspect the symptoms, identify the source of the issue, and explain the best repair option before the problem gets worse.
