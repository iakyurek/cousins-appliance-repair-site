Why Is My Dryer Not Heating?

A dryer that runs without heat can be caused by several common repair issues.

Back to Blog

Lint buildup and airflow restrictions can make a dryer run without drying clothes properly.

Dryer Repair

What this usually means

A dryer that turns on but does not heat can quickly turn laundry day into a headache. The appliance may tumble normally, but clothes stay damp after a full cycle or take much longer than usual to dry.

Common causes include a blown thermal fuse, faulty heating element, bad thermostat, clogged vent, worn belt, or restricted airflow. Lint buildup is especially important because it can reduce performance and create safety concerns if ignored.

If the dryer is gas-powered, heating issues can also involve ignition or gas-related components, which should be handled by a trained professional. Avoid trying to repair gas or electrical components on your own.

A technician can inspect the dryer, test the heating system, check airflow, and determine whether the issue needs cleaning, repair, or replacement parts.
