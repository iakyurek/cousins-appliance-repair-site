FAQ

Quick answers before you book.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the Greater Milwaukee Area, including Milwaukee, Greenfield, Mequon, Franklin, Oak Creek, Wauwatosa, West Allis, Brookfield, Shorewood, Whitefish Bay, Glendale, and nearby communities depending on availability.

Can I book online?

Yes. Use the Book Appointment button to open the Setmore calendar, choose a time, and leave details about the appliance problem.

How does pricing work?

A $95 service fee covers the visit and diagnosis. If you move forward with the repair, that amount is applied toward your final repair cost. Final pricing depends on the appliance, issue, parts, and labor.

Do you offer same-day repair?

Same-day service may be available in many cases, but availability depends on the schedule, location, and parts needed.
