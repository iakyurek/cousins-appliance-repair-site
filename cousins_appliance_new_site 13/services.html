Repair services

Tell us what’s going wrong. We’ll help narrow it down.

Each section explains common symptoms customers search for. When you are ready, book a visit or ask CousinsCare for more details.

Washer Repair

Washer leaking, shaking, not draining, or refusing to start?

We service front-load, top-load, and high-efficiency washers. Common issues include clogged drains, bad pumps, door lock problems, unbalanced loads, broken belts, and unusual noises during the spin cycle.

  • Leaking water or puddles near the washer
  • Washer will not drain, spin, or start
  • Grinding, banging, or shaking during cycles
Dryer Repair

Dryer not heating, taking too long, or making loud noises?

We handle gas and electric dryers. A dryer that does not heat can be caused by airflow problems, lint buildup, heating components, thermostats, fuses, belts, or venting issues.

  • No heat or clothes still damp after a cycle
  • Burning smell, heavy lint, or poor airflow
  • Loud squeaking, rattling, or drum not turning
Refrigerator Repair

Fridge warm, leaking, freezing food, or making loud noises?

A refrigerator problem can quickly lead to spoiled food. We inspect cooling performance, fans, thermostats, leaks, frost buildup, door seals, and other common refrigerator concerns.

  • Not cooling or freezer not freezing properly
  • Water leaking inside or underneath
  • Frost buildup, loud fans, or temperature swings
Oven & Stove Repair

Oven not heating evenly or stovetop not igniting?

We service gas and electric ovens, ranges, and stovetops. Whether the display is dark, burners will not ignite, or temperatures are inconsistent, we can inspect the issue and recommend the next step.

  • Oven will not heat or temperature is inconsistent
  • Stovetop burner will not spark or ignite
  • Control display, switches, or wiring concerns
Dishwasher Repair

Dishwasher leaking, not draining, or leaving dishes dirty?

Dishwasher problems are often tied to drains, hoses, filters, float switches, spray arms, seals, or water inlet issues. We diagnose the source instead of guessing.

  • Water left at the bottom after a cycle
  • Leaking onto the floor or under cabinets
  • Dishes still dirty, cloudy, or not rinsing well
