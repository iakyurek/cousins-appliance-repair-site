Why Is My Washer Not Draining?

Common drainage issues can leave clothes wet and water stuck in the drum. Learn what may be causing it.

Back to Blog

A clogged washer filter or drain path can leave water sitting in the drum.

Washer Repair

What this usually means

If your washer is not draining, it can leave you with standing water, wet clothes, and a lot of frustration. This is one of the most common appliance problems homeowners deal with, and it is usually a sign that something in the drain system needs attention.

Common causes include a clogged drain hose, blocked pump filter, worn drain pump, faulty lid switch, or an overloaded washer. Sometimes the washer will finish the cycle but leave water in the drum. Other times, it may stop mid-cycle and refuse to drain at all.

You can do a quick visual check around the washer for kinks in the hose or obvious blockage, but avoid opening electrical or internal parts if you are not trained. If the washer is repeatedly failing to drain, a technician can inspect the pump, hose, and related components safely.

Catching the problem early can help prevent water damage, extra wear on the machine, and repeat laundry issues. A professional repair visit can identify the exact cause and help get your washer working properly again.
