Dishwasher Not Cleaning Properly? Here’s What It Could Be

If dishes are coming out dirty or cloudy, your dishwasher may need attention.

Back to Blog

Dirty dishes, cloudy glasses, leaks, or standing water can point to a dishwasher issue.

Dishwasher Repair

What this usually means

If your dishwasher is leaving food behind, making dishes look cloudy, or leaving water at the bottom, there may be an issue affecting the wash cycle. A dishwasher should clean consistently without needing multiple runs.

Common causes include clogged spray arms, a dirty filter, drainage problems, low water flow, a worn pump, or a part that is no longer working correctly. Sometimes detergent can play a role, but repeated poor cleaning usually means the appliance needs attention.

Other warning signs include leaking, unusual noises, bad smells, or the dishwasher failing to start. These symptoms can point to drainage, water flow, or electrical issues.

A repair visit can help determine the exact cause and whether the dishwasher needs a simple cleaning, part replacement, or a deeper repair.
